- Do not add years of experience.
- Do not add MW, plant-count, client-count or other statistics.
- Do not add client logos or testimonials.
- Do not add certifications or awards.
- Do not add project details that are not supplied.
- Do not add geographic claims beyond the information supplied.
- Do not invent product capabilities beyond the listed software modules.
- Do not invent performance results or savings claims.
